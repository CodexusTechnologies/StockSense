## About StockSense

**StockSense** is an AI-powered smart inventory monitoring and management system designed to automate inventory tracking, reduce stock management errors, and provide intelligent insights into inventory consumption.

Traditional inventory management often depends on manual counting, spreadsheets, and periodic stock inspections. These methods can lead to inaccurate records, unexpected stock shortages, overstocking, and unnoticed inventory losses.

StockSense aims to solve these challenges through a combination of smart sensor technology, embedded electronics, IoT connectivity, cloud computing, and intelligent data analytics.

Each storage bin is equipped with a smart weighing platform that continuously measures its contents. RFID or QR identification links the bin to a specific inventory item, while environmental sensors monitor storage conditions.

The collected data is processed locally and transmitted to a cloud-based platform, where users can monitor inventory levels, track stock movements, receive alerts, and access consumption predictions.

> **Every bin knows what it holds. Every movement tells a story. Every decision becomes smarter.**

Designed with the vision of being **Made in Sri Lanka**, StockSense aims to make intelligent inventory management more accessible to retailers, pharmacies, manufacturers, and other industries.
